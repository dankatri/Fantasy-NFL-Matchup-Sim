🏈 Fantasy Matchup Simulator

Your fantasy record only tells you who you happened to play. This replays every week against every team in your league — so you can finally prove you were robbed.

Where do I find my league ID?  ·  Why does it say my league is private?

Open your league on fantasy.espn.com. The URL looks like …/football/league?leagueId=203836968&seasonId=2024 — the bold number is your league ID.

This site runs entirely in your browser with no server, so it can only read leagues ESPN serves publicly. If you get a privacy error, a league manager can switch League Settings → Basic Settings → Visibility to Public. Nothing you enter is stored or sent anywhere except ESPN.

Not affiliated with ESPN or the NFL. Data is fetched live from ESPN's public fantasy API and never leaves your browser.
